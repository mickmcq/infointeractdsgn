In class, we did an exercise I'd like you to do when you can. Make a persona poster using Claude or ChatGPT and upload it as a pdf and your prompt(s) as a txt file to Canvas.

# Persona poster

Use a stock photo of a person, not a cartoon.

I want you to use whatever you know or think you know from the contextual inquiry of your project to make a persona poster.

I want you to use visual indicators such as bar charts to depict persona variables.

I also want traditional persona information such as name, age, occupation, etc.

I want a pull quote from the persona.

I want the poster to be attractive enough for developers to want to look at it if they see it in a hallway.

# Then, I want you to upload the prompt(s) and the poster to Canvas.

This exercise serves a couple of purposes. First, it's a chance for you to practice prompting and to improve your prompting by studying the prompting of others and their output. I believe that the persona milestone is one of the few activities in this course that can be well served by genAI, so you should practice to avoid AI slop.

Second, you can improve the persona poster for milestone 3 by making mistakes now and getting ungraded feedback. For example, even though I require a stock photo, the genAI may provide you with a cartoon, which would be downgraded in the actual assignment.
